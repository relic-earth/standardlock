Simple Installation

THREE
STEPS.

No locksmith needed. No special tools. Remove your old deadbolt, fit the Standard, tighten four screws. Done.

01

Remove Old Lock

Unscrew your existing deadbolt — four screws total. Takes about 4 minutes. Works on 99% of standard US door prep.

02

Fit The Standard

Drop the bolt assembly into the latch hole. Feed the connecting bar through and align the rose plates on both sides of the door.

03

Tighten and Test

Four screws. Test the throw. Check the key turns smoothly. Done — you have about 9 minutes to spare.
